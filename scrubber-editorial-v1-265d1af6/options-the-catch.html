Five ways to show "the catch"

The same three problems with commercial scrubbers (size, sound, cost) in five different treatments. Pick one, or mix parts.

1Big-number cards
85lb

They're huge and heavy

A popular professional model, the Abatement PAS1200, stands 39 inches tall, 22 inches wide, and rides on a dolly because nobody wants to lift it. Sized for gutted basements, not the spot beside your nightstand.

65–80+ dB

They're loud

Vacuum-cleaner loud, all day and all night. The crews that run them leave at 5 p.m. You'd be trying to sleep next to one.

$1–2.5K

They cost more than the sticker

Name-brand units run $1,000 to $2,500 before filters, each stage sold separately. No sensors, no auto mode, no homeowner warranty.
