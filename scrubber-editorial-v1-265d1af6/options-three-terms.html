What makes an air scrubber

Three things separate a scrubber from everything else. Here's how each type measures up.

Serious airflow

Enough to cycle a whole room through its filters several times an hour.

  • ✕Air filterOnly while HVAC runs
  • –Air purifierUsually not much
  • ✓Air scrubberA lot

Multi-stage filtration

Big particles, fine particles, and gases each get their own stage, including activated carbon for odors and fumes.

  • ✕Air filterOne sheet, rarely carbon
  • –Air purifierThin carbon layer, if any
  • ✓Air scrubberDedicated carbon stage

Built to run constantly

Metal housings and heavy-duty motors, made to run 24/7 for weeks at a time.

  • ✕Air filterStops when HVAC stops
  • –Air purifierVaries
  • ✓Air scrubber24/7

Who relies on each: air filters are in every home, purifiers are a big-box staple, and scrubbers are what mold, fire, and flood pros bring.
