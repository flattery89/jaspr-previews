From the episode

Start with the free fixes.

Chris asked what people can do without spending anything. Michael's list:

  1. Shoes off at the doorWhatever is on the street ends up in the carpet.
  2. Use the range hoodCooking at high heat fills the house with fumes, even with good ingredients.
  3. Run the bathroom fan after a showerIt's there for moisture, not smell. Give it an hour or two.
  4. Take dirty diapers outsideNot to a pail in the nursery, where the baby sleeps.
  5. Wash it, don't spray itFabric fresheners cover smells. Washing and sunlight remove them.
  6. Open the windowsWhen the weather allows, let the house air out.

Then, for the room you sleep in, a scrubber.
