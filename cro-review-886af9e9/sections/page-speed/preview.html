Frequently asked questions

The number of Jaspr units you'll need depends on your home’s layout, total square footage, and any specific air quality needs. Here are some general guidelines: An open-concept home or condo/apartment usually needs 1 in the family living space. A home with closed off rooms may have one in the kitchen and another in the family living area. We recommend 1 per bedroom for optimal clean air during sleep. If you work from home, then consider 1 in the home office. We offer a discounted Family 4 Pack at checkout.

Our replacement filter subscription ensures your Jaspr air scrubber stays in peak condition without the hassle of remembering to reorder filters. Here's how it works: You'll receive a new filter automatically every 6 months, perfectly timed to keep Jaspr running at peak performance. By subscribing, you save $100 per filter compared to purchasing them individually. No need to track filter life or manually reorder – we take care of everything. You can manage or adjust your subscription at any time through your account. With our subscription service, you can enjoy cleaner air and peace of mind year-round.
