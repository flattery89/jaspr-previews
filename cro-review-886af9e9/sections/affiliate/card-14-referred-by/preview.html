What can Jaspr detect & filter?

Big particles like dust, hair, pet fur, lint, carpet fibers and large pollen.

Traps smells such as from smoke (wildfire, cooking, or cigarettes), and harmful chemical fumes (VOCs) from cleaners, paints, and furniture.

Reduces tiny particles such as fine dust, pollen, allergens, mold spores, and pet dander.
