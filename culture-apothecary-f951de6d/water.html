My Jaspr is green almost all the time. Is it working?

Green means particle levels are low, which is where you want it most of the time. When something changes, like hairspray, cooking or a candle, it turns orange or red, speeds up, and brings the air back to green. The sensor reads particles; it can't detect everything, so it runs and cleans either way.

Why change the filter every 6 months?

The carbon layer works like a parking lot for odors and fumes. Once every spot is full, it stops taking more in, even if the filter still looks clean. Six months keeps it working. A new one ships automatically for $199, and you can pause or cancel anytime.

How many do I need?

One in each bedroom someone sleeps in, plus one for the main living area. For a typical 4-bedroom home, that's about five.

I already have a water filter. Why add this?

They do different jobs. A water filter cleans what you drink. Jaspr cleans the air in the room it's in, all day and night, and speeds up when something like cooking or hairspray hits the air.
