What Jaspr owners say about living with cleaner air at home

Real Jaspr owner reviews on noise, a fan that speeds up on its own, filter changes, looks and service, plus what owners wish were different.

By the Jaspr team[Publish date]5 min read

The work happens where you can’t see it. So what do owners actually notice?

A Jaspr Air Scrubber costs $1,099 , and you can’t watch it work. Being wary of that is reasonable. A green light and a number on a screen aren’t proof.

So instead of telling you what it does, we went back through what owners wrote. More than 1,100 people have reviewed it on jaspr.co . Every quote below comes from one of those public reviews, with the reviewer’s name and the date they wrote it.

We left out any line about how a person feels. A review isn’t a test, and a machine can’t promise a feeling. What’s left is what owners heard, saw and held: noise, a fan that speeds up on its own, a filter at six months, how it looks in a room, who answered the email. And at the end, what owners wish were different, because that’s in the reviews too.

Noise: owners call it quiet

Jaspr runs at 33 dBA on its lowest speed and up to 58 dBA at the top. Penny J. (April 25, 2025) wrote, "The Jaspr is extremely quiet, almost makes me wonder if it’s working..." Josh H. (December 25, 2025) works from a basement office all day: "It runs pretty quietly, which is clutch when I’m on calls or need to focus."

Quiet isn’t silent: at the top speed, 58 dBA, you will hear it.

The fan speeds up on its own

Jaspr has a built-in sensor. When the air in a room changes, the fan ramps up on its own, then settles back down. Cooking comes up most. Robin F. (July 15, 2026) wrote, "It’s amazing how it speeds up during cooking and even when hairspray is used in our bath." Jill R. (March 25, 2026) said it "kicks on quickly after I start drying my hair."

MONICA R. (February 23, 2026) had a piece of bread catch fire in her toaster for a moment. A few minutes later, in her bedroom, her unit was already showing orange and working. Angela O. (July 26, 2025) says it’s "now a game to see who can cook without making the living room Jaspr go crazy."

The filter change is the moment owners remember

It’s one cartridge, swapped whole every six months. It takes under 30 seconds, and there’s no vacuuming. Larson M. (June 23, 2025) wrote, "the filter change could not be easier." Then owners look at what came out. Kim G. (May 24, 2026) : "I just changed the filters and was so surprised at how dirty it was!" Jill R. (March 25, 2026) : "When we changed the filter it was COVERED just like other pictures." Rikka (July 16, 2026) , who also runs one in an RV: "the one we bought in the RV was nasty."

Read a dirty filter carefully. It shows that air moved through it and left something behind, in one home, over a set number of months. It doesn’t say which particles they were, and it isn’t a measure of anything about a person. What it gives you is something you can hold, twice a year.

It looks like furniture and feels like steel

Jaspr is a tall steel tower, about 25 lb, in sand or white, with a green light ring. Setup is plug and purify, with no app required. Owners write about it the way they’d write about a lamp. Bethany W. (January 6, 2026) said, "They are sleek and do not stick out like a sore thumb."

Emily G. (January 21, 2025) noticed the build first: "From the moment we unboxed it, the high-quality build and sleek design stood out." She added that it "doesn’t scream ’medical device.’"

Service: owners write about the people

Megan D. (November 28, 2025) had planned to send her unit back after a change in her life, and reached out to support first. "Not only did he answer all of our questions, he offered to take the unit back if he couldn’t help us out and then he went above and beyond to help us out," she wrote. She kept it. Ann R. (July 29, 2025) wrote, "They stand by their product and are compassionate and quick to respond to emails."

The terms on paper: a 30-day buy-back at the full price you paid, and a lifetime warranty with free shipping both ways and a replacement unit, for as long as you stay on the filter subscription and replace the filter every six months.

Where owners push back: the sensor and the screen

The reviews aren’t all praise, and you should read the rest before you buy. One complaint comes up more than once: the sensor near a humidifier. Adrian C. (February 17, 2026) gave three stars and wrote that "even with a humidifier more than 10 feet away, the air quality indicator shifts to orange or red."

Bethany W. (January 6, 2026) saw the same: the reading rose while a humidifier ran and improved when she turned it off. Gayle D. (March 21, 2022) found that sterile water in the humidifier helped. We won’t guess what the sensor is reacting to . If you run one, expect the reading to move, and try distilled water.

Then the screen. Diana S. (May 11, 2026) gave three stars after her unit showed "Excellent with very low PM2.5" out of the box and kept saying so in a dusty old room. Kathleen M. (December 14, 2024) wrote, "From the second it was turned on, the machine reads ’excellent’ air quality."

The harshest review is one star. SVETLANA J. (November 12, 2025) ran an older unit beside hers in the basement, and just the older unit’s light turned red. She planned to return it, and that is what the 30-day buy-back is for.

A screen that reads Excellent is telling you what its sensor sees at that moment. It isn’t proof the machine works, and we don’t offer it as proof. Look at the filter you pull out at six months, and at the lab report.

Smaller notes, and the price

Christina D. (January 6, 2025) found "the touchscreen is not super reactive." Jeffrey M. (March 13, 2025) called the shipping box "quite heavy and long" for his upstairs apartment. Two owners wanted the light to go darker at night .

Some shoppers ask whether one Jaspr covers a whole house. It doesn’t. It’s sized for a room: the site suggests one per bedroom, one in the living or cooking area, one in a home office or gym. And filters cost $398 a year on the subscription, $598 if you buy them one at a time. Better you hear that here.

The short version: settle it in your own home

Owners describe a machine that’s quiet at the low end, reacts without being asked, shows its work at six months, looks like it belongs, and comes with people who answer. They also describe a screen that confuses some buyers, a sensor that reads humidifiers, and a price that’s real. If you decide it isn’t for you inside 30 days, we buy it back at the full price you paid. Keep the box.
